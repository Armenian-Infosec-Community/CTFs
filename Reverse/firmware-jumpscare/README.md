# Firmware Jumpscare

Every year in Hollow Grove, the Pumpkin King leads the midnight parade wearing his famous badge: a little board with a tiny glowing screen. Nobody knows what it shows. The few who leaned in close to read it ran screaming, and none of them will say what they saw.

This year, someone tried to steal it. In the scuffle the badge hit the cobblestones and the screen went black. The thief got away with nothing but a cracked circuit board, but they managed to pull a copy of the flash before the Pumpkin King's crows came for it.

The screen is dead. The memory isn't. Something is still in there, waiting for someone to look.

Go on. Look closer.

## Notes
`firmware.bin` is a raw flash dump of the badge's microcontroller.
